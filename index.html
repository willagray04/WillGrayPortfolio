Featured project

Sendero Homes · Construction estimating · 2026

An estimating tool that prices a house against real past jobs

Sendero’s cost history lived in a spreadsheet, which meant every new budget started from a rule-of-thumb range. I built an automated estimating tool on top of that history instead: eight completed homes broken into forty cost categories, so a new build gets priced against what the company has actually spent.

Try it. Enter a size and a cost basis, and it allocates the budget the way the database does — each category taking its real share of total spend.
