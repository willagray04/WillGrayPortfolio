Multi-Hazard Exposure and Population Risk Mapping in Brazos County, Texas

Natural hazards are rarely distributed evenly inside a single county, but FEMA’s National Risk Index reports wildfire, extreme heat, and flooding separately rather than as one comparable picture. This project combines all three at the census-tract level to find where exposure is highest, and how much danger that puts critical infrastructure and population centers in.

I am the team contact and project lead, responsible for acquiring the hazard, population, and infrastructure data.

  • Three hazards combined — wildfire, extreme heat, and flooding — normalized into a single composite risk score for each census tract.
  • Built in Python using pandas and geopandas, with a script that automates map production so different hazard weightings can be compared quickly.
